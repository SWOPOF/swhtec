# What is PT100?

PT100 is a type of Resistance Temperature Detector (RTD) made from platinum, widely used in industrial temperature measurement for its high accuracy and stability.

## Key Features

- High precision
- Stable performance over wide temperature range
- Suitable for industrial automation
- Long-term reliability

## Applications

- Process control
- HVAC systems
- Laboratory equipment
- Industrial machinery
