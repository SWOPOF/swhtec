# RTD Wiring Guide

Proper wiring of RTD sensors is critical for accurate temperature measurement.

## Wiring Methods

- 2-Wire: Simplest, but less accurate due to lead resistance
- 3-Wire: Most common for industrial use, compensates for lead resistance
- 4-Wire: Most accurate, used in precision instrumentation

## Installation Tips

- Keep wiring short and shielded
- Avoid parallel runs with high-power cables
- Use proper connectors for industrial environments
