# Temperature Sensor FAQ

## Common Questions

**1. What is the accuracy of PT100 sensors?**  
- Typically ±0.1°C to ±0.3°C depending on class.

**2. What temperature range can PT100/PT1000 handle?**  
- Standard: -50°C to 250°C; high-temp variants available up to 600°C.

**3. Can the sensor length be customized?**  
- Yes, standard lengths are available and custom lengths can be ordered.

**4. Can these sensors interface with PLCs?**  
- Yes, compatible with most PLC analog inputs using proper signal conditioning.

**5. What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) and delivery time?**  
- MOQ: 1 piece for standard types; Delivery: 3–5 days for in-stock, longer for custom models.
