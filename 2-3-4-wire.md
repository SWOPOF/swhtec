# 2-Wire vs 3-Wire vs 4-Wire RTD

Different wiring schemes affect measurement accuracy and suitability for applications.

## Comparison

| Wiring Type | Accuracy | Application |
|------------|----------|-------------|
| 2-Wire     | Low      | Short-distance, low-cost |
| 3-Wire     | Medium   | Industrial standard |
| 4-Wire     | High     | Precision measurement, lab equipment |

## Recommendation

- Use 3-Wire for most industrial environments
- Use 4-Wire for critical temperature control systems
