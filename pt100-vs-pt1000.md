# PT100 vs PT1000

Both PT100 and PT1000 are platinum RTD sensors, but they differ in resistance value, sensitivity, and application scenarios.

## Differences

| Feature        | PT100          | PT1000        |
|----------------|----------------|---------------|
| Nominal Resistance | 100Ω          | 1000Ω        |
| Sensitivity       | Lower          | Higher       |
| Cable Length Impact | Less sensitive | More sensitive to long cables |
| Common Application  | General industrial use | Precision measurement, long cables |

## Choosing the Right Sensor

- Use PT100 for standard industrial processes
- Use PT1000 for high precision or long-distance wiring
